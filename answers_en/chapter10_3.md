# 10.3 Enterprise AI Platform Design — Questions & Answers

**1. How Can AI Products Enter the Enterprise Market (B2B)?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Enterprise customers place greater emphasis on data security/privacy compliance, integration capabilities with existing systems, customizability, and customer support. The product needs to invest in these capabilities accordingly. Additionally, sales cycles are typically longer, requiring dedicated sales and service support systems.

</details>

**2. How Can AI Products Enter the Consumer Market (B2C)?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Consumer users focus more on instant gratification, ease of use, and price sensitivity. It is necessary to minimize friction (e.g., no-login trials, clear freemium tiers) and achieve low-cost acquisition through word-of-mouth and social sharing.

</details>

**3. How Can AI Products Approach Internationalization?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Internationalization goes beyond language translation; it requires considering local user habits, compliance requirements (such as data privacy regulations), localized partners, and payment gateways in different regions. It is generally recommended to deeply validate in one overseas market before expanding incrementally.

</details>
