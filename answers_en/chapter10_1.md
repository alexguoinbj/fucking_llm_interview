# 10.1 PMF (Product-Market Fit) — Questions & Answers
> [⬅ Back to Index](../README_EN.md) · Chapter 10: LLM Product Deployment and Commercialization · 3 questions

**1. How Can AI Products Identify Real User Needs?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Deeply observe the current actual workflow of target users and identify the time-consuming, repetitive, error-prone, yet necessary steps. These steps are often where real needs lie, rather than imagining out of thin air "what cool things AI can do."

</details>

**2. How Can AI Products Find Suitable Application Scenarios?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Start from the pain point where "existing solutions offer a poor user experience, but the demand is real," match the advantages of AI technology (generation, understanding, automated execution) relative to traditional solutions, and confirm the intensity and frequency of demand for the scenario through small-scale validation.

</details>

**3. How Can AI Products Achieve Product-Market Fit (PMF)?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Through rapid iteration and small-scale user testing, closely observe the specific scenarios in which users frequently and spontaneously use the product and are willing to recommend it to others. Focus on and reinforce these scenarios with clear signals, rather than pursuing broad, all-encompassing feature coverage.

</details>
