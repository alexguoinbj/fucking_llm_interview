# 10.6 GPU Cost Optimization and Inference Cost Control — Questions & Answers

**1. How Can AI Products Control Operating Costs?**

<details>
<summary>💡 Show Answer</summary>

Control unit costs through model routing (routing simple tasks to smaller/cheaper models), effective caching strategies, limiting unnecessary multi-turn or multi-agent calls, and performing targeted optimizations for high-frequency scenarios (e.g., fine-tuning a small model to replace frequent large model invocations).

</details>
